CALENDAR STANDARD

One official clock. Astronomical boundaries. Civil days.

Cena Standard Time is permanently fixed at UTC+09:00 and never observes daylight saving time. The CST civil day containing the Winter Solstice is Ardanna and begins a Wholga. The final day of Elcetre is Arrovona. Each Molo begins on the CST civil day containing a New Moon, and Carpanya marks the Summer Solstice.

Wholga 1 contains 6 August 1945; earlier years are numbered as Morgra. Astronomy calculated with Astronomy Engine.
