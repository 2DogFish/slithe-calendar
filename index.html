CALENDAR STANDARD

One official clock. Astronomical boundaries. Civil days.

Cena Standard Time is permanently fixed at UTC+09:00 and never observes daylight saving time. The CST civil day containing the Winter Solstice begins a Wholga; the CST civil day containing a New Moon begins a Molo.
